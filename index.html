Victoria Bikes started in late July 2025, when our founders were moving through a hard season in their lives. Getting on their bikes and riding together brought them peace and light when they needed it most.

What began as a way to get through those days grew into a community. Now we make room for others to find that same fresh air, connection, and joy. You don’t need custom lights to belong here—just come ride with us.

Meet the VTX Ryders crew

GOOD PEOPLE.
GREAT RIDES.

A few bikes, a hard season, and the people who kept showing up for each other. Meet the crew that helped turn those first rides into a community.

Co-founder • Bike whisperer

Kenny.

Bikes, bright lights, and Broncos football—that’s Kenny’s kind of lineup. He helps people find the bike that fits them, fixes what needs fixing, and keeps the crew rolling. He rides sport bikes too and can happily talk MotoGP for hours. There’s always room for a little football talk between rides.

Co-founder • Website & software wizard

Toni.

Builds websites and software, then trades screen time for ride time—with plenty of Swiftie energy along the way. She’s here for fresh air, good company, and memories on two wheels. Taylor Swift has her heart; MotoGP has her attention.

Co-founder • Pace setter

Meemaw & Finn.

Three wheels, working blinkers, and no plans to be last. Meemaw loves her daughters, grandkids, bingo, and a break to scratch a lotto ticket. Finn, her dog, rides in the back basket. Watch out—this duo can move.

Co-founder • Wizard on Wheels

Nick.

Here from the beginning, with Pokémon cards to collect, trade, and sell—and enough Harry Potter enthusiasm for the whole crew. He rides sport bikes, loves MotoGP, and brings a little wizarding-world energy to the group.

Core Crew • Chief Hype Officer

Adrianna.

The person behind the posts, messages, and ride updates—powered by Starbucks and a good playlist. Add a nap and some true crime, and you’ve got her ideal downtime. If you message the page, chances are you’re talking to her.

Core Crew • Gears & Cheers

Linda.

Texans football, a Monster in the white can, and a ride with good people—that’s Linda’s kind of day. She’s learning the bike ropes as she goes and loves taking care of Adrianna. Adrianna has the posts covered; Linda’s got Adrianna covered.

Before you roll

A few things
to know.

How do I find the next ride?+

We ride near-nightly in Victoria. Check our Facebook page for the latest meetup details before heading out. Message the page if you’re new or have questions.

Do I need custom lights to ride?+

No! You don’t need LED wheel rings or a custom lighting setup to join us. The group is about riding together and enjoying good company. Bring your bike and come be part of it.

Are the rides family-friendly?+

Yes! Family-friendly rides are at the heart of Victoria Bikes. Check the current meetup details and message us about what to expect before bringing the family.

How did Victoria Bikes get started?+

Our founders started riding together in late July 2025 during a hard season in their lives. Biking brought them peace and light when they needed it most. That connection grew into the community we share today.

Do you also help with custom lights?+

Yes! Custom lights are optional—you don’t need them to ride with us. For lighting information, email us at hello@victoriabikes.com or message us on Facebook.
