Before you roll

A few bright
answers.

What can you put lights on?+

We do custom plasma LED installs on BMX bikes, cruisers, e-bikes, Harleys, and golf carts. Tell us about your ride and the look you want so we can talk through the options.

How much does a custom install cost?+

Each setup is different. Send us your vehicle type and whether you’re thinking wheel lights, frame lights, or both. We’ll discuss the details and provide a custom quote.

How do I find the next ride?+

Check Victoria Bikes on Facebook for current ride updates and meetup details, or call (361) 212-1219. Check the latest details before heading out.

Are the rides family-friendly?+

Yes—family-friendly night rides are part of what we do. Contact us about the next outing so you can find out what to expect before bringing the family.
